Design notes

Phone shop — catalog, cart & checkout

The Shop page lists each phone variant with a real product image, price, stock count and an Add to Cart control. Clicking a product opens a spec modal. The header cart icon opens a drawer with quantity controls, customer details and a Pay with PayFast button that submits a server-signed payment form.

Design notes

Support — log a ticket

The Support form captures full name, email, phone, ticket type, subject and message. The data is submitted to /api/v1/tickets and stored with a generated ticket ID. Success and error states are handled with clear user feedback.

Technical / Billing / Sales / General

Design notes

Sign up — service lead capture

The Sign-up form pre-fills the service of interest from query parameters (e.g. after clicking a package link). It posts to /api/v1/leads/signup and stores the lead for the sales team to follow up.
Pre-fill
?service= query support
Backend
POST /api/v1/leads/signup
Fields
Name, email, phone, interest, location
CTA
Direct to WhatsApp fallback
